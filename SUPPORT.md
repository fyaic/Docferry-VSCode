# Support

For product, account, billing, or privacy help, open **DocFerry: Open
Dashboard** and choose Support, or email `support@bondie.io`.

For reproducible extension defects that do not contain private workspace data,
open an issue at https://github.com/fyaic/Docferry-VSCode/issues.

Include the extension version, VS Code version, operating system, Remote/SSH or
local workspace mode, the action attempted, and the redacted message from the
**DocFerry** output channel. Do not post session tokens, private share links,
customer identifiers, card information, source documents, or provider keys.

Security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.
